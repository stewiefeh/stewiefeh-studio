How a project runs

  1. 1You show me the business

    Your services, hours, photos, and the one thing you want visitors to do: call, book, order, or walk in.

  2. 2I build a sample

    A working draft of your own site, with your name and colors on it. Not a mockup, not a stock template.

  3. 3You react

    You look it over and tell me what to change. Revisions are part of the plan, not an extra bill.

  4. 4It launches

    Live on your domain, search-ready, and you get support after launch while it settles in.
